# M14: Food on the table

**Shipped (player-visible):** the camp starts with 400 meat in its food store. Each Sunday morning families walk to the
camp and fill a week's larder. Households eat daily, and a short larder shows **Hungry** (nothing else changes). The top bar
reads "Food: N days" with counts per type. The house panel shows the larder and days left; the citizen panel shows Hungry.
The build bar's **Forager** entry builds a foraging camp: foragers aged 8+ pick berries and mushrooms into the hut store or
a built granary. The build bar's **Granary** entry places an 8 × 6 m granary with a 5 m yard, plus its panel.

**Design sources:** `design/food.md` (FD-01..32, FOOD-A*), `design/tuning.md` (Food, Foraging). Systems: `systems/food.md`,
`systems/foraging.md`.

## Technical changes by system

- **Inventory:** `Items` Meat, Berries, Mushrooms (Food category). `LocationKind.FoodStore`. `Ledger.AddFoodStore`, `FindFoodStore`.
- **Food (new, `Sim/Food/`):** `AllotmentSystem`, `FoodTripSystem`, `EatingSystem`, `Larders`, `HouseholdLarder`, `FoodStats`, `IFoodMarket`, `GranaryReport`.
- **Foraging (new, `Sim/Foraging/`):** bush records and registry, `ForagingSystem`, `ForagingStores`, `ForagingStatus`, `BushRegrowSystem`. `SimWorld.Bushes`.
- **Tasks:** `task.fetch_food` (owner family) and `task.forage`. Steps `CollectFood`, `DepositFood`, `DepositForage`. `DispatchSystem.RunFamilyTrips`.
- **Operations and footprints:** foraging operation and areas. Granary operation with no area (`AreaId` 0). `PlaceStructure` command,
  `StructurePlacement`. Granary and forager hut module types.
- **Homes and start:** move-in and eviction move larder food. `StartLayoutApplier` stocks the starting meat and creates larders.
- **Save:** Bushes section (id 10), Food section (id 9). Forager tuning in Core. `SaveWriter.CurrentVersion` 11.
- **Tuning:** food and foraging fields in `SimTuning`, and a "Food and foraging" group in `SimConfig` (T-426).
- **Unity:** `FoodText` in the top bar. House and citizen panels show larder and Hungry. `OperationPanelView` forager lines.
  `GranaryPanelView`. `StructureTool` (granary). `OperationDraftTool` forager id. `BushRenderer`. `ModuleRenderer` draws standalone
  structures. `AgentPicker.TryPickGranary`. `GameSceneGenerator` wires all of these.

## Decisions

- 0041: the granary is an operation without an area, so families assign to it with the existing commands.
- 0042: food is ledger stock in larders; the week's allotment is a reservation; the Sunday trip is a task.
- 0043: foragers deliver to the nearest store with room, never the camp; each basket is a ledger item on a carrier.
- Owner decisions dated 15/11/2026 in `design/food.md`: FD-12 (camp stop-gap), FD-16 (Hungry has no effect), FD-23 (age 8),
  FD-23b (hut store), FD-30b (5 m yard).

## Known gaps and debt

- **Save coverage:** `SimSaveRoundTripTests` has no food, bush or granary case, and there is no `FoodSectionTests` (decision 0034).
- **Granary UI:** the panel has no Assign or Unassign control and no id in its title (FD-31, FD-32). Picking a granary opens
  the granary panel, not the operation panel, so no UI assigns families to it.
- **Design drift:** `design/tuning.md` gives the granary as 6 logs. Code and FD-30 use 8.
- **Camp market:** the camp food store is never refilled and does not spoil (FD-12, FD-17).
- **Trip target:** `FoodTripSystem.OpenTrip` always walks to the camp, not to `IFoodMarket.Origin`.
- **Config:** `SimConfig` module recipes are "NOT YET APPLIED", so editing the granary recipe there does nothing.
- **Values:** foraging and granary numbers are placeholders (FD-20..30).
- **Storage yard:** still greyed out on the build bar.
