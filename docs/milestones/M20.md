# M20: Thatch

**Shipped (player-visible):** the build bar places a Thatcher's hut: draw the thatcher's area, then the hut (8 logs, 480 worker-minutes).
Reed beds are scattered on low, flat ground. A thatcher (age 14+) cuts bundles that cure 7 days where they stand. Dry sheaves
are carried home one per trip into the hut store (40). Winter cutting is 4 times faster than other seasons. The panel shows
status, store, stooks curing, next ready day, dry sheaves and a greyed "Grown grain: Needs farming". A house now costs
10 logs, 10 thatch and 10 wattle panels. The build bar greys out a short house, and the plot is refused. Both name the
short item ("Not enough thatch (have 0, need 10)"). A new game starts with 90 thatch and 90 panels in the camp's General store.

**Design:** `design/materials.md` (MAT-30..40b, MAT-43), `design/planks.md` (PL-11), `design/homes.md` (HM-01, HM-06 notes),
`design/storage.md` (ST-42b). **Systems:** `systems/thatch.md`, `systems/homes.md`, `systems/construction.md`,
`systems/start-layout.md`, `systems/tasks.md`, `systems/save.md`, `systems/unity-ui.md`, `systems/unity-visuals.md`.

## Known gaps and debt

- **Hut tuning is not read.** `ThatcherHutLogs`, `ThatcherHutMinutes`, `ThatcherHutWidthM` and `ThatcherHutDepthM` are saved and
  shown in SimConfig, but `ModuleTypes.ThatcherHut` hard-codes 8 logs, 480 min and 6 × 4 m. Same debt as M19 weaver and wattle.
- **House recipe is hard-coded** in `ModuleTypes.House` (10, 10, 10, 720). This breaks the SimTuning rule.
- **Grown grain never available:** the Sim has no fields, so the toggle stays greyed.
- **No carts:** thatch goes by hand, 1 a trip (MAT-35's cart of 5 is not built).
- **Art:** stooks are primitive cones. Ricks (MAT-33) are OPEN. Reed only: river rush (MAT-30) is not modelled.
