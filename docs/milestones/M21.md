# M21: Tier 1 houses: daub, fences, levels 1-2

**Shipped (player-visible):** a daub pit on the build bar makes 10 daub a day (store 20). Houses cost 10 logs, 10 thatch,
10 wattle panels and 10 daub, and the camp starts with 90 daub. A built house offers **Build fence** (wattle panels, 1 per
2 m of fenced edge). A built fence is solid, and the road frontage is the open gate. The house panel shows the household
level and a needs checklist. With 2+ food types in the last Sunday allotment, not hungry and a built fence, **Upgrade**
appears and makes the household level 2. The top bar tooltip counts households by level. Upgrade is free, and level 2 gives nothing yet.

**Design:** `design/household-levels.md` (HL-*), `design/materials.md` (MAT-41, MAT-42), `design/placeholder-art.md`.
**Systems:** `systems/homes.md`, `systems/workshops.md`, `systems/footprints.md`, `systems/construction.md`,
`systems/navigation.md`, `systems/food.md`, `systems/save.md`, `systems/unity-ui.md`, `systems/unity-visuals.md`.

## Technical changes by system

- **Workshops and items:** `Items.Daub` (General, hand load 2). `WorkshopDef.Daubing`: no input, 10 a day, store 20.
- **Footprints:** `ModuleTypes.DaubPit`. The house takes 10 daub. `ModuleTypes.Fence` marker (not in `All`). `HouseNoDaubRecipe` for pre-daub sites.
- **Areas and homes:** `FenceLine.Of(plot)` (derived, never saved). `HomeRecord.FenceFootprintId` (saved). `HouseFence`.
  `HouseRemoval.RemoveWithPlot` takes the fence with the house.
- **Commands:** `BuildFence`, `UpgradeHousehold`.
- **Navigation:** a built fence blocks the `NavGrid` cells along its line. `NavSyncSystem` recomputes them on fence changes.
- **Levels:** `HouseholdLevels`, `NeedDef`. `Family.Level` (saved, never drops). `Family.AllotmentFoodTypes` (set by
  `AllotmentSystem`, saved in Food). `HouseholdNeedsReport`, `HouseReport`, `HouseholdLevelCounts`. Decision 0061.
- **Tuning:** `StartingDaub`, `HandCarryDaub`, `DaubPerDay`, `DaubPitStore`, `FencePanelLengthM`, `FenceMinutesPerPanel`,
  `DiverseFoodTypes`. Mirrored in `CoreSection`, the SimConfig asset and tests.
- **Save:** version 37 to 41. v38 daub tuning and starting daub, v39 fence link, v40 family level, v41 allotment food types.
- **Unity:** `daub-pit` build-bar entry. `HousePanelView` (level, needs, fence, Build fence, Upgrade). Top bar tooltip.
  `ModuleRenderer.AddFences`. Placeholder art and its wiring in `GameSceneGenerator`.

## Decisions

- **0053** A fence is its own footprint (built here).
- **0061** Level is stored, needs are derived on read, and only `UpgradeHousehold` raises it.
