# M17: Timber chain, beams and planks (MVP-4 part 1)

**Shipped (player-visible):** the village turns logs into planks. The player places a hewing station and a sawpit from the build bar and assigns a family to each. Hewers fetch logs by ox and hew 3 beams a day. Sawyers fetch beams by ox and saw 3 beams a day into 9 planks. A new game starts with 10 planks in the camp General goods store and no beams. A market stall costs 2 planks, carried by hand by its granary family; a granary without planks shows *Waiting for planks*. The top bar shows Food, Logs, Beams and Planks. Clicking a hewing station or sawpit opens the workshop panel.

**Design:** `design/planks.md` (HW-*, PL-*), `design/storage.md` (ST-02), `design/markets.md` (MK-21). **Systems:** `systems/workshops.md`, `systems/construction.md`, `systems/inventory.md`, `systems/markets.md`, `systems/tasks.md`, `systems/operations.md`, `systems/unity-ui.md`, `systems/save.md`.

## Technical changes by system

- **Items and ledger:** `Items.Plank` and `Items.Beam` (General). `LocationKind.GoodsStore` (General only, refuses logs and food) and `WorkshopStock` (input only, not a construction source). Save version 28 (T-512, T-513, T-520).
- **Construction:** `SiteMaterials` reserves every recipe item nearest-first, all or nothing, and gives the shortfall text. `SiteRecipe` keeps the log recipe for an old stall that holds logs. Progress and deconstruction refunds cover every recipe item (T-514, T-515, T-516).
- **Hand carry (T-517, T-518):** other items than logs go to the site on foot, `HandCarryPlanks` (2) per trip, as `task.carry_materials`. A stall's carry goes to its granary family (assigned, age 8+), not to odd-jobbers.
- **Workshops (T-520 to T-524):** `WorkshopDef` (hewing: log to beam; sawing: beam to 3 planks; numbers from `SimTuning`). One `WorkshopSystem` opens `task.fetch_input` (ox, after construction) and one `task.workshop_work` per working family, at `shift ÷ inputs per day`. `InputSources`, `WorkshopBuildings`, `WorkshopStatus`, `WorkshopReport`, `GoodsStats` (read only).
- **Placement:** `PlaceStructure` also places the hewing station and sawpit (6 × 4 m, 6 logs, 480 worker-minutes), each with a 1-slot operation.
- **Markets:** stalls use `SiteMaterials`; `StallSystem` skips a family with fewer than 2 planks and marks it for *Waiting for planks* (T-519).
- **Unity:** build-bar entries `hewing-station` and `sawpit` (`ToolEntry`, `StructureTool`); `AgentPicker.TryPickWorkshop`; `WorkshopPanelView` (T-527); timber labels in `TopBarController` (T-527); `ModuleRenderer` draws beam and plank stacks by fill (T-526); four placeholder visuals; `SimConfig` "Timber chain" group (T-528).
- **Save:** version 29 (the workshop tuning was added in 29 and the workshop stock location in 28).

## Known gaps and debt

- Input reserve (PL-30 to PL-33, M18) is not built. Hewers take every log they can reach.
- A sawpit holds 18 planks, not 20: a beam makes 3, so it stops with 2 slots left (PL-06).
- Stall planks: a carried load that is cut short at sunset drops on a site-owned pile and is not reserved again if no spare planks exist.
- Stalls placed before T-519 keep their log cost (PL-13 note in the design doc).
- Not built: storage yard (ST-*), tier 2 houses that use beams (PL-11b), artisan steps (PPL-42), walk-to-site for builders.
- Stall builds need an odd-jobber family; with every family assigned, build tasks wait.
