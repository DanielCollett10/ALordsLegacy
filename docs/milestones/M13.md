# M13: Homes

**Shipped (player-visible):** the build bar has **House plot**, enabled once logs cover the house recipe (10 logs). Draw a
plot of 60–400 m² with road frontage; a 6 × 8 m house appears on the frontage edge as a site. Odd-jobbers haul logs and
build it. When it is Built, one Homeless family moves in: the tent at the camp goes, and the family sleeps and mills about
the house. Clicking a house or its plot opens the house panel (state, household, members, High priority, Deconstruct).
The citizen panel shows "Home: <family> house" or "Homeless (camp)". Deconstructing a house evicts its family to the camp
fire, and the plot goes with the house.

**Design sources:** `design/homes.md` (HM-01..22), `construction.md` (CN-50), `building-tool.md` (BT-02/03), `tuning.md`.
Technical shape: decision 0040, plus the save section in 0034.

## Technical changes by system

- **Areas:** `area.plot` type (60–400 m², frontage required); `AreaValidator` refusals "A plot must face a road" and
  "Too small for a house"; `HousePlacement` (pure, longest frontage edge, 1 m back).
- **Homes (new, `Sim/Homes/`):** `HomeRegistry`, `MoveInSystem` (operations phase), `HouseRemoval` (Evict, RemoveWithPlot).
  `DesignatePlot` adds plot, site and Empty home in one Apply (T-405).
- **Footprints:** `ModuleTypes.House` (6 × 8 m, 10 logs, 720 worker-minutes).
- **Construction:** house deliver and build tasks use owner -1 (`DeconstructionWork.HouseOwnerId`) and pool oxen (T-443).
  Marking a house evicts; finishing or cancelling removes plot (T-407).
- **People:** `Family.HomeHouseId`, `IsHomeless`; housed families mill and commute from the house access point (T-406).
- **Save:** `HomesSection` is id 8, written after Population. `SaveWriter.CurrentVersion` is 5 (decision 0034).
- **Tuning:** `SimConfig` synced to M13 Sim defaults (T-411); `design/tuning.md` has the house rows.
- **Unity tools and UI:** `PlotTool` (build bar `house`, T-408); `AgentPicker` selects houses; `HousePanelView` (T-410);
  citizen home line (T-410).
- **Visuals:** `visual.module.house` placeholder (generated, `VisualSizes.ModuleHouse`); `CampRenderer` draws tents only
  for Homeless families and rebuilds on `Homes.Version` (T-409).
- **Scene:** `GameSceneGenerator` wires the house panel and plot tool; `Game.unity` regenerated.

## Decisions

- 0040: a house leaves with its family (evicts), and its plot goes with it; a plot with a house cannot be deleted.
- 0034 / 0038: the Homes section and the version bump, in the same task as the state.
- Owner decisions in the design: HM-06 (10 logs, 720) and HM-11 (who moves in) are DECIDED on 15/11/2026.

## Known gaps and debt

- **Tent spot not saved:** an evicted family's home is the camp fire, so its tent is drawn there (see `design/homes.md` HM-14 note).
- **No family choice:** the auto move-in rule is the only way a family gets a house (HM-11 "later").
- **Placeholder numbers:** house size, plot limits (HM-03, PROPOSED), recipe 720 min (CON-A1).
- **No walk to site:** builders work where they stand (CN-23, carried from M10).
- **Save coverage:** no `HomesSectionTests`, and the round trip has no house with a family (decision 0034 requires it).
- **Extra plot space** is only garden; no backyard use (HSE-03/04 later).
- **Test evidence:** no `docs/test-runs/M13-*.md` in the repo, so the T-412 and T-413 results are not recorded here.
