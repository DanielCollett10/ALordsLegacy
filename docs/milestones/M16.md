# M16: Growth (MVP-3 part 1)

**Shipped (player-visible):** the village has a loop. With an empty house and a month of spare food, a new family walks in along the trunk road from the map edge and moves in. A family hungry 30 days in a row walks out to the nearest road exit and disappears. A message line under the top bar says who came and who left, and fades after 30 s. Citizen and house panels show *Hungry (n / 30 days)*, *Arriving* or *Leaving (hungry)*. The top bar shows citizens, with families, arrivals, departures and the next-family reason in its tooltip. The default trunk road runs to the nearest map edge.

**Design:** `design/growth.md` (GR-*), `design/tutorial-notes.md` (TUT-02, TUT-03). **Systems:** `systems/growth.md`, `systems/people.md`, `systems/homes.md`, `systems/food.md`, `systems/start-layout.md`, `systems/unity-ui.md`.

## Technical changes by system

- **Growth (new `Sim/Growth/`, `Sim/Arrivals/`):** `DepartureSystem`, `FamilyDeparture`, `ArrivalSystem`, `ArrivalRules`, `GrowthReport`, `FamilyGenerator`, `ArrivalCatalog` (T-502, T-505, T-503, T-506). Routine order: Eating, Departure, Arrival.
- **Passage (T-498, T-499, T-500):** `Family.Passage` and `IsInPassage`. Work, routine, dispatch, allotment, food trip, stall, store-family and move-in readers skip passage families. `AssignFamily` refuses them.
- **Map exits and entry (T-497, T-507):** `MapExits` (derived, never saved). `StartLayoutApplier` sets `SimWorld.EntryPoint` through `MapExits.ComputeEntry`. `StartLayoutGenerator` extends the trunk road to the nearest reachable edge.
- **Hunger (T-496):** `EatingSystem` counts `Family.HungryDays` and sets `World.LastHungryTick`.
- **Homes (T-504):** `HomeRecord.ReservedForFamilyId`. `MoveInSystem` never gives a reserved house to anyone else.
- **Settlement log (T-498):** `SettlementLog` on `SimWorld`, last 20 `(tick, text)` entries.
- **Ox load (T-495, T-510):** `SimTuning.OxLoadLogs` defaults to 1 and is exposed in `SimConfig`. Shared `CarrierDrop` helper, moved out of `ForagingSystem`.
- **Unity:** `MessageLogView` and `MessageLogFade` (T-508). Population readout and tooltip in `TopBarController` (T-509). Status lines in `CitizenPanelView` and `HousePanelView` from `GrowthReport`. `PeopleRenderer` drops removed citizens (T-509). `GameSceneGenerator` wires the log (T-510).
- **Save:** `SaveWriter.CurrentVersion` is 27. Passage, settlement log, reservation, last arrival, entry point and hunger fields were added in versions 22 to 27 (`systems/save.md`).

## Decisions

- **0045 Arrivals and departures:** passage state, derived map exits, saved entry point, house reservation, arrival food as new supply, settlement log.
- **Owner, 16 and 17/11/2026** (in `design/growth.md`): hunger never slows work (GR-01); leave after 30 hungry days (GR-03); arrival food and surplus rules (GR-20 to GR-22); trunk road to the map edge (GR-24b); no POP-A6 (GR-25); message log under the top bar (GR-30).

## Known gaps and debt

- Arrival food is put in the family larder at spawn; the walkers do not carry it in (GR-22b). See the design note.
- Names and age ranges are code defaults in `ArrivalCatalog`, not start-layout data (GR-23). See the design note.
- The leaving message uses the actual hungry-day count, which is 30 at default tuning (GR-14). The fade starts at 25 s (GR-30). The top bar shows citizens only (GR-31). See the design notes.
- The end-to-end test builds its village by hand, not through `StartLayoutApplier`.
- The nearest-walkable-edge fallback exists for maps with no road to the edge (GR-24b).
- Not built: POP-A6 (adults join incomplete families), homeless departure (GR-13b, CMP-26), deaths, births, player alerts (FG-10).
