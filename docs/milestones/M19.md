# M19: Coppice, weaver and wattle

**Shipped (player-visible):** the player draws a coppice area and places a coppice hut from the build bar. Hazel and willow
stools are scattered at a new game (40 and 20). A cutter (age 8+) takes up to 10 rods a trip into the hut's own store (40 of
each kind). A cut stool regrows after 30 days. The build bar also places a weaver (6 willow rods make 1 basket) and a wattle
yard (6 hazel and 2 willow rods make 1 panel). Both are staffed like workshops and hand-fetch their rods. The storage yard
collects their baskets and panels. Forager huts (5 kit slots) and granaries (2) claim baskets, and their own family fetches
them. A carrier with a borrowed basket carries twice as much (forage 10 to 20, granary food 10 to 20). The operation panel
shows the hut's rods line and "No stools"; the granary panel shows "Baskets: n / m".

**Design:** `design/materials.md` (MAT-01..25, MAT-50..54), `design/storage.md` (ST-20..25, ST-42b). **Systems:**
`systems/coppice.md`, `systems/kit.md`, `systems/workshops.md`, `systems/foraging.md`, `systems/inventory.md`,
`systems/footprints.md`, `systems/operations.md`, `systems/storage.md`, `systems/tasks.md`.

## Technical changes by system

- **Bushes:** `BushKind` gains `HazelStool` and `WillowStool`. `BushScatter` places hazel stools at the forest edge and willow
  stools on `LowGround`. Decision 0050.
- **Coppice:** `Coppice/` (`CoppiceSystem`, `CoppiceStores`, `CoppiceStatus`, `CoppiceReport`, `CoppiceDrop`); `task.coppice`.
- **Items and ledger:** rods, `Basket`, `WattlePanel` (General). Per-item caps on a goods store (`Ledger.SetItemCap`, T-555).
  `HandLoads`: rods 10, baskets 5, panels 2.
- **Workshops:** a recipe holds several inputs (or none), one `WorkshopStock` per input, hand inputs through
  `task.haul_by_hand`, and a fetch choice by lowest share. Weaver and wattle yard defs. Decision 0051. Save v35 (per-input stock).
- **Kit:** `Kit/` (`KitStores`, `KitClaims`, `KitFetchSystem`, `KitLoans`). A loan is a reservation. Decision 0052. Save v36 (claims).
- **Read models:** `CoppiceReport`, `WorkshopReport.Inputs`, `GoodsStats.Rods/Baskets/Panels`, `ForagerHutReport`, `GranaryReport`.
- **Tuning:** `SimConfig` group "Coppice, weaver and wattle"; the asset values equal the code defaults.
- **Unity:** build-bar entries `coppice`, `weaver`, `wattle-yard`; stool drawing in `BushRenderer`; weaver and wattle boxes in
  `ModuleRenderer`; `GameSceneGenerator` wires the tools; `PlaceholderVisualGenerator` adds the two module ids.
- **Save:** version 36 at the end of M19 (`SaveWriter.CurrentVersion` is now 37, set by the thatch work).
