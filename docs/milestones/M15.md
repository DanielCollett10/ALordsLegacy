# M15: Markets (completes MVP-2)

**Shipped (player-visible):** the build bar's **Market** tool draws the town's one market area (100 to 2,000 m²) with
stall slot outlines. The granary panel's **Supplies market: Yes / No** switch hands the camp's food over to the market.
Each family assigned to a granary gets a 3 × 2 m stall on the nearest free slot. Those families collect food from forager
huts into the granary and carry it by hand to their stall. On Sunday, households within 630 m walk to the stalls for their
week's food. The camp disappears at midnight once everyone is housed; leftovers become a ground pile. The market panel
lists stalls and stock and can deconstruct an unstaffed stall. The house panel shows **Market access**. Plots and fixed
structures get real outlines, and the granary ghost snaps flush to plots and roads.

**Design sources:** `design/markets.md` (MK-*, owner decisions 16/11/2026), `design/camp.md` (CMP-20..25), `design/food.md`
(FD-12). Systems: `systems/markets.md`, `systems/food.md`, `systems/foraging.md`.

## Technical changes by system

- **Markets (new, `Sim/Markets/`):** `MarketRegistry`, `MarketRules`, `StallSystem`, `StoreFamilySystem`, `StallMarket`,
  `MarketRange`, `MarketReport`; commands `SetGranarySupplies`, `DeconstructStall`; `Start/CampRemovalSystem`.
- **Geometry and placement:** `StallGrid` (slots from the polygon), `FixedPlots`, `PileZones`, `StructureSnap`. Fixed
  structures get a plot outline, and plots may not overlap (T-471).
- **Food and foraging:** `AllotmentSystem` and `FoodTripSystem` pick camp or market; `SimWorld.FoodHandedOver` is set once.
  Foragers deliver only to their own hut store (T-454).
- **Tasks and operations:** `task.stock_stall` (`StepRunnerSystem.Stock`). `HaulSystem` hauls stranded partial log piles
  (T-490); the operation panel says why logs are not hauled (T-491).
- **Footprints and save:** stall type in `ModuleTypes` (3 × 2 m, 1 log, 60 min, 30 food). Markets section (id 11), Food
  section extended, `SaveWriter.CurrentVersion` 19.
- **Unity and editor:** `MarketTool`, `MarketPanelView`, granary and house panel lines, picking, `SnapMarkers`,
  `PlacementOverlay`, market/stall/crate visuals, `CampRenderer` (camp goes). `PlaceholderVisualGenerator` and
  `GameSceneGenerator` wire them.

## Decisions

- 0044: only store families move goods from production into stores; foragers never carry to a granary.
- 0048: stall slots are derived from the market polygon, not saved.
- Owner decisions 16/11/2026 in `design/markets.md`: one market per town (MK-04), full stall families (MKT-03b),
  deconstruct an unstaffed stall for now (MK-23b).

## Known gaps and debt

- **Range tint** shows only while a market is selected, not while placing (note on MK-41).
- **Stall padding:** `StallGrid` keeps a 2 m edge strip the design does not mention (note on MK-05).
- **Camp meat:** "Not in M15" says camp meat stays out of granaries, but the code empties the camp store into the
  granary after the handover (note in `design/markets.md`).
- **Granary recipe:** `design/tuning.md` says 6 logs; code and FD-30 say 8.
- **Config drift:** `Assets/Tests/EditMode/SimConfigTests.cs` expects 700 m and 12 carry items; `SimConfig.asset` has 630 and 10.
- **Save coverage:** no food, bush or granary case in `SimSaveRoundTripTests`; no `FoodSectionTests`. Market bytes are
  checked only by `MarketEndToEndTests` (decision 0034).
- **Placeholders:** market area, stall size and food, 630 m range, 10-item carry.
- **Not built:** storage yards, spoilage, towns and several markets, set dressing, reachable-ground overlay.
