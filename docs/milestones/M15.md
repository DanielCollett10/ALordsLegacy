# M15: Markets (completes MVP-2)

**Shipped (player-visible):** the build bar's **Market** tool draws the town's one market area (100 to 2,000 m²) with stall slot outlines. The granary panel's **Supplies market: Yes / No** switch hands the camp's food over to the market. Each family assigned to a granary gets a 3 × 2 m stall on the nearest free slot, and those families collect food from forager huts and carry it by hand to their stall. On Sunday, households within 630 m walk to the stalls for their week's food. The camp disappears at midnight once everyone is housed; leftovers become a ground pile. The market panel lists stalls and stock and can deconstruct an unstaffed stall. The house panel shows **Market access**. Plots and fixed structures get real outlines, and the granary ghost snaps flush to plots and roads.

**Design:** `design/markets.md` (MK-*), `design/camp.md` (CMP-20..25), `design/food.md` (FD-12). **Systems:** `systems/markets.md`, `systems/food.md`, `systems/foraging.md`.

## Technical changes by system

- **Markets (new, `Sim/Markets/`):** `MarketRegistry`, `MarketRules`, `StallSystem`, `StoreFamilySystem`, `StallMarket`, `MarketRange`, `MarketReport`; commands `SetGranarySupplies`, `DeconstructStall`; `Start/CampRemovalSystem`.
- **Geometry and placement:** `StallGrid` (slots from the polygon), `FixedPlots`, `PileZones`, `StructureSnap`. Fixed structures get a plot outline; plots may not overlap (T-471).
- **Food and foraging:** `AllotmentSystem` and `FoodTripSystem` pick camp or market; `SimWorld.FoodHandedOver` is set once. Foragers deliver only to their own hut store (T-454).
- **Tasks and operations:** `task.stock_stall` (`StepRunnerSystem.Stock`). `HaulSystem` hauls stranded partial log piles (T-490); the operation panel says why logs are not hauled (T-491).
- **Footprints and save:** stall type in `ModuleTypes` (3 × 2 m, 1 log, 60 min, 30 food). Markets section (id 11), Food section extended. `SaveWriter.CurrentVersion` was 19 when M15 shipped.
- **Unity and editor:** `MarketTool`, `MarketPanelView`, granary and house panel lines, picking, `SnapMarkers`, `PlacementOverlay`, market, stall and crate visuals, `CampRenderer` (camp goes). `PlaceholderVisualGenerator` and `GameSceneGenerator` wire them.

## Decisions

- 0044: only store families move goods from production into stores; foragers never carry to a granary.
- 0048: stall slots are derived from the market polygon, not saved.
- 0049: the Sunday food source switches once, at the handover, and never mixes.
- Owner decisions 16/11/2026 in `design/markets.md`: one market per town (MK-04), full stall families (MKT-03b), deconstruct an unstaffed stall for now (MK-23b).
