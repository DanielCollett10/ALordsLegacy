# M18: Storage yard

**Shipped (player-visible):** the player places a storage yard from the build bar (8 logs, built by workers) and assigns up to
two families to it. Those families carry the camp's General goods into the yard, pick up loose goods, and collect planks from
workshop output stores. Beams come by ox from hewing stations. Sites and sawpits draw from the yard. Each hewing station and
sawpit has an input reserve the player sets on its panel (default 5). The yard panel shows stock, status, families and a greyed
Supplies market toggle ("No demand yet"). The camp General store closes once a yard exists, and the camp goes when everyone is
housed and food is handed over, leaving its goods for the yard. The top bar Beams and Planks readouts carry a "General goods" tooltip.

**Design:** `design/storage.md` (ST-*), `design/planks.md` (PL-30..34). **Systems:** `systems/storage.md`, `systems/workshops.md`,
`systems/tasks.md`, `systems/inventory.md`, `systems/operations.md`, `systems/save.md`, `systems/unity-ui.md`, `systems/unity-tools.md`.

## Technical changes by system

- **Yard planner:** `YardFamilySystem` plans `task.haul_by_hand` from an ordered `YardJobs.Standard()` list of `IYardJobSource`s:
  camp store, piles, workshop output, beams by ox. Stock and room are reserved at planning (ST-44). Reads `YardRoute`.
- **Ledger and items:** `GoodsStore` for yards (General only). `Items.Plank`, `Beam`, rods, `Basket`, `WattlePanel`. `HandLoads.For` per-item loads.
- **Workshops:** `InputReserve` (storage-only count, PL-31) gates fetches. `SetWorkshopReserve` (0..9999). `WorkshopReport` shows
  "Waiting: N / R logs in storage". Input stocks 10, hewing beam store 10.
- **Read models:** `StorageYardReport` (ST-45 status, ST-10 text, village totals via `GoodsStats.GeneralByItem`).
- **Camp:** `CampRemovalSystem` closes an empty camp General store (ST-04), holds the camp while its goods have no yard (ST-30, message ST-32), drops goods as a pile (ST-31).
- **Tuning:** `StorageYard*` (size, 8 logs, 480 min, 300 capacity, 2 families), `YardCollectThreshold` 0,
  `WorkshopDefaultReserve` 5, `HandCarry*` for rods, baskets, panels and items. `SimConfig` group "Storage yard (M18)".
- **Save:** version is 36 (M17 ended at 29). Tuning in Core, per-operation reserves in Structures, goods stores in the Ledger.
- **Unity:** `StorageYardPanelView`; `StructureTool` id `storage-yard`; `ToolEntry` enabled; `AgentPicker` picks yards in the
  workshop slot; `WorkshopPanelView` reserve field; `UiFocusRules.AllowTextInput` lets only the reserve box take focus, on click (T-602);
  `ModuleRenderer` draws the yard placeholder (8 x 0.8 x 6 m); `GameSceneGenerator` wires the panel.
- **Editor:** `PlaceholderVisualGenerator` adds `visual.module.storage_yard` (row in `design/placeholder-art.md`).

## Decisions

- **0054** Yard jobs are an ordered list of sources; the planner holds no rules.
- **0055** The input reserve counts storage only and is set per workshop.
- Owner, 17 and 18/11/2026 (in `design/storage.md`): yard capacity 300, collect any free workshop output (no half-full rule), camp goes with a yard.
